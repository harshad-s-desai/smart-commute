How Smart Commute Works

Experience smooth carpooling in 3 easy steps. Whether you want a ride or have empty seats to share.

01

Sign Up & Verify

Create your verified profile in seconds using your mobile number and college or office details for complete safety and trust.

02

Find or Offer Rides

Search active rides heading your way or post your own route with flexible timings, seat count, vehicle type, and fair cost-sharing.

03

Travel Together

Coordinate smoothly, meet at your designated Kolhapur landmark, travel comfortably, and split fuel expenses effortlessly.
